# Abstract
This illustrative paper considers how conversion of agricultural land near cities is recorded, and how outdated land-use entries contribute to disputes.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Findings
In peri-urban districts, agricultural land is often converted in practice before the land-use entry in the record is changed. This gap between recorded and actual land use complicates valuation, taxation and compensation, and increases the chance of competing claims. Satellite change detection can flag parcels where land use appears to have changed without a matching record update.
