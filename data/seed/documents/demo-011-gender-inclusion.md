# Abstract
This illustrative paper asks whether record digitization improves or weakens women's recorded land rights.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Findings
Digitization can make joint ownership and inheritance shares visible when records are updated to reflect them. However, digitizing existing records without correcting past omissions reproduces earlier exclusion of women from the record. Programs that included a targeted correction drive for inheritance entries recorded higher shares of women as recorded co-owners.

## Access barriers
Online services can favour applicants with digital access and literacy. Assisted service points and multilingual interfaces were associated with more equal use of online record services.
