# Scope
This illustrative review considers whether modern survey methods, including drone imagery and satellite-aided mapping, reduce boundary disputes.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Findings on mapping approaches
Boundary disputes arise mainly where parcel boundaries were never surveyed to modern standards or where field boundaries moved without an update of the sketch. High-resolution imagery makes it faster to produce parcel maps, but maps only reduce disputes when neighbouring owners accept the mapped boundary. Participatory verification, in which owners confirm boundaries in the field, was associated with fewer subsequent objections.

## Relationship with record digitization
Digitizing textual records without updating parcel geometry leaves boundary disputes largely untouched. Linking each digitized record to a verified parcel geometry is the step most often cited as reducing boundary-related disputes.
