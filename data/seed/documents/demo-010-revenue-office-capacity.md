# Findings
Implementation capacity, meaning trained staff, working equipment and clear procedures, is the main factor separating offices that benefit from digitization from those that do not.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Workload during transition
During rollout, workload can temporarily rise because offices run manual and online processes in parallel and reconcile data. Offices with low capacity experienced longer transition periods and larger backlogs. Phased rollout with dedicated support teams reduced this transitional burden.

## Sustaining gains
Once systems stabilized, offices reported lower processing pressure per request. Gains were retained where training was repeated for new staff and where a helpdesk resolved technical problems quickly.
