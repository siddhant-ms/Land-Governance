## Registration versus mutation
Registration of a transaction and mutation of the revenue record are separate legal steps with different effects. Registration creates public notice of a transaction, while mutation updates the revenue record used for tax and administrative purposes. Neither, by itself, always establishes conclusive ownership.

## Presumptive record systems
Record systems in which entries are presumed accurate but can be challenged are described as presumptive. Digitizing a presumptive record improves access and traceability but does not change its legal presumption. Reforms toward conclusive titling raise different questions about state guarantees and compensation for errors.
