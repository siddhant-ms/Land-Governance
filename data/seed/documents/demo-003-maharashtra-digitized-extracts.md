# Overview
This illustrative case describes online availability of digitized land extracts and an automated mutation workflow in a large state. It is a demonstration document for the evidence repository and is not a real evaluation.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Access to records
Making digitized extracts available online reduced visits to revenue offices for certified copies. Applicants reported that access to a current extract before a transaction reduced uncertainty about who held title. Lenders and registration offices increasingly relied on the digital extract as a first check.

## Workflow automation
Automatic triggering of mutation after registration shortened the gap between a sale and the update of the record. Offices noted that the automation worked well for straightforward sales and poorly for partition and inheritance cases, which still required manual verification.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Effect on disputes
The illustrative case notes that disputes stemming from delayed mutation, where a buyer's name did not appear in the record for months, became less frequent after automation. Disputes involving inheritance shares and shared holdings persisted. The authors emphasize that the evidence is descriptive and that no comparison group was available.
